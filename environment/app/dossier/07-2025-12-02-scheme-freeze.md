## Scheme decisions

### GW-009 - SUPERSEDED

GW-009 is reversed. The body-hash field is ALWAYS the lowercase hex SHA-256 of the body bytes. An empty or absent body therefore hashes the empty string, giving `e3b0c44298fc1c149afbf4c8996fb92427ae41e4649b934ca495991b7852b855`. The `UNSIGNED` sentinel is retired; it was an unnecessary divergence from the AWS-style scheme and cost us 2 days of partner debugging.

_This replaces the GW-009 entry in the decision register. The register is not edited; this note is authoritative._

### GW-023 - SUPERSEDED

GW-023 is widened. Restricting the signed set to `x-gw-*` left `content-type` and `host` unprotected, which the December threat review flagged as a downgrade vector. EVERY header present on the request is now signed, with the single exception of `authorization`, which carries the signature itself and can never be part of its own input.

_This replaces the GW-023 entry in the decision register. The register is not edited; this note is authoritative._

### GW-052 - SUPERSEDED

GW-052 is corrected. The trailing newline was a transcription error carried over from the internal design doc; the reference signer never emitted one. The canonical request is the 6 fields joined by LF with NO trailing newline.

_This replaces the GW-052 entry in the decision register. The register is not edited; this note is authoritative._
