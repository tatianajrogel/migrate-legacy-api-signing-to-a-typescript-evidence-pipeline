## Scheme decisions

### GW-041 - SUPERSEDED

GW-041 is reversed. The fronting proxy re-indents folded header values before they reach us, so 'exactly as received' is not stable. Header values are now whitespace-folded before signing: strip leading and trailing spaces and tabs, then collapse every internal run of spaces and tabs to a single space.

_This replaces the GW-041 entry in the decision register. The register is not edited; this note is authoritative._
